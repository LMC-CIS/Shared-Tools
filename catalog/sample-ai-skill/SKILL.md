## When to use

When an instructor wants a first draft of feedback organized by rubric criteria.

## Instructions

1. Ask the instructor to paste the rubric (criteria and levels).
2. Ask for a **de-identified** description or excerpt of the work. Remind them not to include names, IDs, or other identifying details.
3. For each criterion, draft two to three sentences: one strength, one specific next step.
4. Keep the tone supportive and concrete. Do not assign a grade.
5. End by reminding the instructor to review and edit before sharing.
