About

Academic Tools in Development is a public hub where college faculty and staff share small tools and resources they have built for teaching, advising, and running courses.

What you will find

  • Bookmarklets: one-click helpers that run on a web page you already use.
  • AI skills: instruction files that guide an AI assistant through a specific task.
  • Guides: short how-to documents.
  • Workflows: step-by-step processes, sometimes with templates.
  • Apps and browser extensions: always shared as source code, never as ready-to-run programs.

Who runs it

The site is a community effort led by Lake Michigan College CIS faculty. A small group of volunteer maintainers reviews submissions and keeps the catalog tidy. It is not an official college product and is not endorsed by the college or by any software vendor.

What “working project” means

Every resource here is shared as-is by the person who made it. Some are polished; many are experiments. Each listing shows its status, when it was last tested, and when its link was last checked, so you can judge for yourself. See the disclaimer.

Status labels

Active
Working and looked after by its author.
Experimental
Early or in progress. Expect rough edges.
Unmaintained
No longer updated. It may still work, or may not.
Retired
No longer recommended. Kept for reference and hidden from the list by default.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, cookies, tracking, or analytics. Your light/dark choice is saved only in your own browser.
